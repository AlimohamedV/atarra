The problem

Weed control is reactive, and reactive is expensive

The ecological cost

Phragmites australis forms dense monotypic stands that displace native macrophytes, cut light penetration and degrade the habitat value of Ramsar-listed wetlands.

The hydraulic cost

In the Delta's canal network, reed beds choke cross-sections. That is lost conveyance for the Ministry of Water Resources — irrigation capacity physically removed from the system.

The operational cost

Surveying is boat-and-eyeball, per canal reach, per season. Coverage is sparse, timing is late, and by the time a stand is visible from the bank it is already expensive to harvest.

The intervention is never the hard part — the detection and the prioritisation are. Which canal reaches are infested, how fast is each advancing, and where does the next pound of budget do the most good?

Satellite revisit is 5 days and the archive is free back to 2022. The data to answer that question continuously already exists. What is missing is a pipeline that turns it into a defensible number.
What this is

A GeoAI platform, not a script

ATARRA finds, tracks and ranks Phragmites infestations across Egyptian waterways from free multispectral imagery — so maintenance shifts from seasonal dredging to early, targeted intervention.

1 · Track

A cloud-masked, single-grid time series of multispectral composites, read window-by-window from public cloud-optimised GeoTIFFs. No imagery store, no licence, no account.

2 · Analyse

Weakly-supervised labels and a multispectral U-Net produce per-pixel reed masks; a geographically-isolated test split is what makes the resulting metric worth quoting.

3 · Recommend

Growth curves and expansion vectors feed a blockage-threat score and a harvest-window optimisation — prioritising reaches, not just reporting them.

Why the zone role is modelled

Wetlands are managed for habitat and open-water exchange; canals are managed for cross-section capacity. The same infestation carries a different cost in each, so the decision engine needs the distinction as data rather than as a footnote.

Why one lake is not one scene

Burullus spans four MGRS tiles on different relative orbits — 36RTV covers 51%, 36RUV 34%, 36STA 14%, 36SUA 23%. A single-date mosaic therefore has genuine holes; a first attempt reached only 57% coverage. Compositing over a ±3-day window reaches 100%.

Data source

Sentinel-2 L2A — free, open, and served by byte range

Verified reachable, not assumed

The Element 84 Earth Search STAC API serves sentinel-2-l2a over the Delta continuously from 2022 through 2026. A single August 2023 search over Burullus returned 29 scenes under 10% cloud.

Read with HTTP ranges, never downloaded

Band files are Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFFs on public S3. A full 10 m band is ~68 MB, but the files advertise Accept-Ranges: bytes. A 512×512 window is therefore ~0.5 MB of transfer.

This is the design decision that made the whole project possible on a laptop with under 3 GB free: the pipeline never needs to store imagery to use it.

The band contract

Visible bands are 10 m; red-edge and SWIR are 20 m. Everything is resampled onto one canonical grid before stacking — a tensor whose channels sit on different pixel grids is a silent correctness bug, not a design choice.

The SCL cloud mask is resampled nearest-neighbour. Interpolating class codes invents classes that do not exist.
Data source · a real finding

The archive's metadata contradicts its own pixels

Sentinel-2 reflectance can be stored two ways. The collection-level metadata says one thing and the item-level metadata says the other. Both cannot be right — so the pixels were measured directly.

ConventionBurullus: negative pixelsDeep ocean NIR
DN / 10000 used0.0%+0.032
(DN − 1000) / 10000 rejected74%−0.068

Reflectance cannot be three-quarters negative, and deep clear water cannot have negative NIR. DN / 10000 is the validated convention.

Why this is not a footnote

A wrong convention does not raise an exception. It silently biases every spectral index, and therefore every label the rule engine produces and every number downstream.

So the assumption is re-checked at runtime

validate_reflectance() runs on each composite and flags anything above a 2% negative-pixel fraction. If the archive ever changes processing baseline, the pipeline announces it instead of drifting.

This is the project's recurring principle: an assumption that cannot fail loudly is not an assumption, it is a latent bug.

The grid is the contract

Every layer agrees on one raster grid, defined in core/grids.py. Two dates one pixel out of phase would fabricate change in a time series and offset every segmentation mask. AOI bounds are snapped outward to multiples of the ground sample distance, landing on the lattice Sentinel-2 itself uses.

Status discipline

complete means implemented and exercised end to end. scaffolded means the interfaces, contracts and algorithms are real and unit-tested, but nothing reachable calls them yet. The two are never blurred.

Reserving before training is the whole point

A test set the model has already seen is not a test set. --exclude-pack removes the pack's ground — the same reed bed on every date it was imaged, plus neighbours within half a tile — rather than only the tile keys it names.

Staged deliberately, for free-tier reality

Colab sessions disconnect when idle. A fetch combined with a training run means a single disconnect discards both. Splitting build from train means the expensive artefact survives.

Build is not resumable and neither is a training run — which is exactly why the store exists as a separate, cacheable stage.

Labels

Weak supervision — because no labelled reed dataset exists

The constraint

There is no public per-pixel Phragmites ground truth for the Nile Delta. Hand-labelling enough imagery to train from scratch is a season of work, not a semester.

The approach

A spectral rule engine in datasets/weak_labels.py derives training labels from NDVI / NDWI / NDRE / NDMI and the SCL mask. It then records every pixel it declined to be confident about.

That uncertainty mask is not discarded — it is the annotation worklist.

The caveat, carried in the artefact

A metric computed against these labels scores agreement with the rule engine. If the rules are wrong about a pixel, a model that reproduces them faithfully is still scored correct. Every metrics.json carries that as a field, so the number cannot be quoted without it.

Two bugs worth stating

Review mask ≠ loss mask. Reusing the review threshold as a training filter deleted the entire cropland class by 0.003 of confidence, silently turning a 4-class problem into a 2-class one. Ambiguity is decided on margin instead — 0.067 for a genuine reed/crop coin flip versus 0.23–0.52 for real decisions.

Method

One model, two arms, and only one variable between them

Multispectral U-Net

A self-contained encoder-decoder in pure PyTorch — no timm, no segmentation_models_pytorch. 8 input channels, 4 semantic classes: open water, crops/soil, mixed halophytes, Phragmites australis.

The RGB control arm

3 channels from the same store, same splits, same seed, same augmentation. This is how the proposal's "multispectral gain" claim gets substantiated instead of asserted.

Half-tile stride

--stride 128 overlaps tiles 2× for roughly 4× the training samples at no extra download cost, because imagery is fetched once and cut afterwards.

A subtle correction that changed a published number

build_model(variant="rgb") used to override the caller's normalisation statistics with hard-coded constants. That quietly made every reported gain part band set and part preprocessing — two variables moving together. The constants are now only a fallback; each arm is normalised from its own training split.

Class imbalance is handled, not ignored

Reed is ~1.8% of pixels on real Burullus imagery, so unweighted cross-entropy is minimised by predicting "not reed" everywhere. Inverse-frequency loss weights are necessary — and are computed from the training split alone, so validation balance never leaks into a training decision.

Results · one held-out tile

What the model actually sees

A real Sentinel-2 tile from the held-out test split, 20 m, 14 July 2026 — the imagery, the supervision it was trained against, and what it predicts. Nothing here is a mock-up, and the true colour is stretched to 0.35 reflectance so that 0.3% of pixels clip rather than 58%.

It reproduces the teacher where it was taught

Reed agreement on this tile is IoU 0.93, against 0.91 pooled over the whole test split — so the tile is representative rather than flattering. It sits at the median of the reed-bearing tiles.

The labeller could not see the water

The water rule rested on NDWI, and over turbid lagoon water NIR comes back at 0.94× green, so NDWI lands at +0.03 — the wetness term contributes nothing exactly where the class matters. Worse, the crop rule paid out a flat 0.30 for the absence of a red edge, which is not evidence of cropland at all, so water (0.35) and crops (0.30) tied inside the ambiguity margin and the lagoon was dropped from the loss.

Fixed, and measured on the rebuilt 273-tile store: +2,431,960 px of supervised ground recovered (+24.8%), held-out water ground up from 22,167 px to 811,600 px, and the reed class untouched — 415,905 px either way.

What is still unsupervised, the model guesses

Grey is 53% of this tile: the ambiguity filter still withholds the ground where two rules nearly tie, and the model fills those pixels with its best guess. Weak supervision bounds what the model can learn, which is what the reserved annotation tiles are for — not a modelling problem, a labelling one.

The conclusion, stated precisely

At two dates the RGB arm's Phragmites IoU of 0.003 means it never learned the class at all. At twelve dates it reaches 0.677 from the same bands.

So "how much do the four extra bands buy" is scale-dependent in magnitude — 0.28 IoU at twelve dates, near-total at two. The honest claim is that 8-band beats RGB at both scales, not that it buys a fixed number of points.

The error this avoids

Quoting only the 12-date gap would present a 0.28 improvement as the value of multispectral imagery. Quoting only the 2-date gap would present a near-total advantage. Both are the same table read dishonestly — and it is the same class of error the scoring guards exist to catch.

A related trap: support size

Reed is ~1.8% of this imagery, so a test set drawn without regard to class can contain almost none of it. One arbitrary annotation selection reported "reed IoU 0.0" from 36 support pixels — a number that measures nothing. It is why --strategy reed exists, and why the scorer refuses below a 500-pixel floor.

Two parameters carry more weight than they look

--gsd 20

Keeps twelve dates to 1.9 GB of composites. At 10 m it is 7.6 GB, which does not fit a free-tier session. --gsd 20 is not a quality compromise, it is the difference between running and not running.

--stride 128

Overlapping tiles are separate samples for the loss, which is the point — but they must never straddle the train/test fence.

Engineering integrity

Six decisions that decide whether the number means anything

1 · Split by geography, and verify it

Adjacent tiles are autocorrelated and, with stride 128, literally overlap. Partitions are defined on the ground, boundary tiles are dropped, and every run calls assert_disjoint on the result.

2 · Report the checkpoint you kept

train() restores best.pt before final validation and before the test pass. Otherwise the run selects an epoch on validation and then reports whichever epoch happened to run last.

3 · Nothing held-out touches a training decision

Loss weights, normalisation statistics and augmentation all come from or apply to training tiles only. Validation and test views are built with augment=False.

4 · Seed before construction

set_seed runs before build_model, and loader shuffling uses an explicit generator — the global RNG's state depends on how much randomness model construction happened to consume.

5 · Fail on empty supervision

A batch with no supervised pixel is skipped; an epoch with no such batch fails; non-finite losses abort. One NaN batch would otherwise write NaN into every weight while the loss curve kept printing.

6 · Checkpoints carry provenance

Ordered band names and every split's tile keys. A channel count cannot tell B08 from B04 — the shape matches and every number is plausible.

Fifteen such rules are documented in the README, each with the failure it prevents. Several regression tests carry a negative control — the superseded behaviour reproduced and asserted to fail the same check — because a guard that cannot fail tests nothing.
Engineering integrity · worked example

The scorer refuses to flatter you

Observed during development

A model trained for 2 epochs on 7 tiles reported reed IoU 1.0, "proposal targets met" — because the annotation covered a single class, and a model predicting that class everywhere scores 1.0 on every pixel of it.

Arithmetically perfect. Completely empty. This is the failure mode that motivated the whole integrity layer.

Three ways a report is rejected as a validation

  • Fewer than two classes annotated
  • Fewer than 500 reed pixels annotated
  • The model predicts a single class across every annotated pixel

The independent test set

Building a test set a panel will accept

The rule engine already knows which pixels it cannot call — so annotating its uncertainty is cheaper and more informative than annotating at random. 

atarra annotation export data/dataset --out data/annotation --limit 20 --strategy reed
atarra train  data/dataset --exclude-pack data/annotation     # reserved ground stays unseen
atarra annotation score data/annotation --checkpoint data/checkpoints/<run>/best.pt

What the export writes

Three georeferenced GeoTIFFs per tile — the imagery, the rule engine's current guess (so you correct rather than start from blank), and a review mask — plus annotation.geojson with lon/lat corners per tile.

Why --strategy matters

reed picks tiles richest in the target class, so a reed IoU is actually measurable. The default uncertainty builds a hard-case set — defensible, but not a representative sample.

QGIS is the annotation tool

Labels are polygons with an integer class_code. Geometry is reprojected from WGS84 into the chip's CRS before rasterising — rasterising lon/lat against a UTM transform does not fail, it silently produces garbage.

A 12-date pack of 8 tiles covering 257,352 reed pixels was exported and reserved before training. Those tiles are still unlabelled — and that single fact is what stands between this project and a defensible accuracy claim.

Limitations

Stated plainly rather than discovered by the panel

  • No field validation. Labels are bootstrapped by weak supervision and need a verification pass before reported accuracy means anything.
  • Every accuracy figure is agreement with the rules, not field-verified detection. The 8-band arm reaches 0.958 Phragmites IoU against that teacher — past the 0.82 / 0.85 thresholds — and the artefact still records the verdict as unearned.
  • No model trained to convergence. The 12-date arm's best validation epoch was the last one; it was still improving when training stopped, so 40 epochs is a budget, not a converged setting.
  • The notebook itself has never been executed on Colab. The pipeline was driven on a live T4 from a scratch notebook using the same CLI commands in the same order. That exercises the runtime, install, GPU and download — the Drive mount and the notebook's own cell ordering remain unexercised.
  • --strategy reed biases the test set on purpose toward the class the proposal quotes a number for. That makes the reed IoU measurable; it also means the set is not a representative sample, and those are two different claims.
  • Weak class. mixed_halophytes sits at 0.524 IoU against Phragmites at 0.958 — a genuine and unexplained gap.
  • Store builds and training runs are not resumable. An interrupted build restarts; there is no checkpoint-resume path. A dropped free-tier session costs the whole run.
  • Biomass is a proxy, not a measurement. Canopy biomass cannot be observed directly from orbit; the harvest-window logic integrates NDVI/NDRE over a season as a documented proxy.
  • The CDSE adapter is unexercised end-to-end — it needs a real account and downloads ~800 MB per product. STAC is the default for that reason.
  • The GPU CI job has no runner, so CUDA and AMP coverage still only exists on one machine.
The habit worth noting: each of these is written into the README and into the machine-readable artefact it affects, so a score cannot be quoted without its caveat travelling with it.
Next steps

What closes the remaining gap

Immediate

Label the 8 reserved tiles in QGIS, then run annotation score. That is the one step that converts 0.958 into a defensible number — everything needed for it already exists and is exported.

Short term

Reserve a second, random-strategy pack for a representative figure alongside the reed-biased one; extend training past 40 epochs since the best epoch was the last; and close the mixed_halophytes gap.

Then

Bring forecast/ and decision/ onto the critical path — growth curves, expansion vectors, blockage threat — and surface the whole thing through the MapLibre dashboard as an operational tool rather than a research artefact.

What the project demonstrates

A complete, free, reproducible geospatial ML pipeline — data acquisition, weak labelling, geographically-honest evaluation, dual-arm ablation, and a deployment surface — built and run end to end on infrastructure that costs nothing.

What it demonstrates beyond that

An unusual amount of attention to whether the number is true. Fifteen documented design rules, a regression suite whose subject is score validity, negative controls on the guards, and an artefact that refuses to print a verdict it has not earned.
